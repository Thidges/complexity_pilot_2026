Preface: All monetary amounts in this study are shown in Experimental Currency Units (ECU), which will be converted to {{ real_world_currency_code }} at the end of the study with an exchange rate of {{ exchange_rate }}.

Environment: You are part of a closed ring system with {{ group_size }} participants (including yourself), as illustrated below. Hence, you have a direct predecessor and a direct successor, with other participants located in between.


{% if group_size == 5 %} {% else %} {% endif %}

Situation: Each participant has their own inventory that stores units of a virtual good. At any time, you can request one unit of this virtual good from your predecessor. You receive a unit only if your predecessor has at least one unit in their inventory. Similarly, your successor can request a unit from you at any time.

Inventory: Your inventory can be zero or positive. Every time you receive a unit from your predecessor, your inventory increases by one. Every time your successor receives a unit from you, your inventory decreases by one.

Your actions: To request a unit, click “Request a unit” in the left box on the decision screen. The box turns blue for a second when your predecessor’s inventory has a unit, and you receive it; otherwise, it turns orange.

Your successor's actions: When your successor requests a unit, the right box turns blue for a second when your inventory has a unit and your successor receives it; otherwise, it turns orange.

Costs and Benefits:

  • You start with {{ ecu_endowment }} ECUs.
  • You earn {{ ecu_earn }} ECUs every time your successor receives a unit from you.
  • You must pay {{ ecu_inventory_cost }} ECU every second for each unit in your inventory, i.e., holding inventory is costly.
    • {{ if ecu_request_cost > 0 }}
  • You must pay {{ ecu_request_cost }} ECU for every request you make to your predecessor, irrespective of whether it results in you receiving a unit or not.
    • {{ endif }}
  • Your ECU balance adjusts accordingly. Technically, it can go negative, but you can also recover.

Duration: The task ends after {{ round_seconds }} seconds ({{ round_minutes|to0 }} minutes).


Training: To get comfortable with the situation, you have a {{ training_round_seconds }} second round without payments, where a computer predecessor and successor click randomly. After the training, all participants in the ring are the humans identified in the Zoom meeting.

Payment: Your payment after the experiment is the fixed fee plus your total ECU transferred to Euros, given the exchange rate {{exchange_rate}}. If your total ECU is negative, you only receive the fixed fee.
