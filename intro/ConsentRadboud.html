Consent Form

Purpose: This study examines how people make decisions in a complex system.

Procedure: You will participate in an experiment with other anonymous participants. In a computer environment, you and others will make decisions that affect your monetary consequences from this experiment. During the experiment, you will 1) fill in this consent form, 2) read and understand the introductions for the tasks, 3) undergo a non-paid training task to comprehend the task better, 4) participate in an interactive task that determines your bonus, 5) fill in a short questionnaire, and 6) provide your bank details for the payment.

Benefits: When completing the study, you receive a fixed payment and a bonus payment determined by your actions in the interactive task.

Risks: We do not foresee any physical or mental risks. You are free to leave the study at any time. Note that completion of the experiment is necessary to receive a payment.

Personal data: We use anonymous raw data from your decisions for analysis and publication in a scientific article, as well as for placement in a publicly accessible data repository. These data sets cannot identify you.
We need your first and last name and IBAN for the payment. Therefore, you will leave the experimental environment to store such data on a separate, GDPR-compliant server based in the EU. Hence, your personal information is decoupled from your choices in the experiment and your answers to a questionnaire. Your total payments will be transferred within three weeks by the finance department of Radboud University.

Data retention period: Research data underlying a scientific publication (thus not your personal data used for payment) will be retained for at least 10 years after publication, in accordance with the research data management policies of the universities involved in this research project.
